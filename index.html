Commands

Everything the bot does today. Type / in any channel the bot can see.

League of Legends

/lol vincular
Link your Riot account by typing your Riot ID.
/lol perfil
Level and ranked info for you, a member, or any Riot ID.membro · riotid
/lol partida
The live game as one image: every player with rank, win rate or ARAM numbers, and mastery.membro · riotid
/lol desvincular
Remove the link between your Discord and your Riot account.

Music

/play
Play a song or playlist from YouTube in your voice channel.busca
/queue
Show what is playing and what comes next.
/skip
Skip the current song, or jump to a position in the queue.para
/pause
Pause or resume.
/seek
Jump to a point in the song, like 1:30.tempo
/remove
Remove a song from the queue.posicao
/stop

How we use Riot data

DarknessBotAPPToday 21:15

DarknessBot reads public League of Legends data from the Riot Games API, on BR1, only when someone runs a command. This is the full list.

APIs and what each one is for
Riot APIUsed for
account-v1Find an account by Riot ID and keep the Riot ID up to date.
summoner-v4Summoner level and profile icon.
league-v4Rank, LP, wins and losses in Solo/Duo and Flex.
spectator-v5Find the game a player is in right now.
champion-mastery-v4Mastery on the champion each player picked.
challenges-v1ARAM and Arena rank, ARAM wins and pentakills.
  • Stored: your Discord user ID linked to your Riot PUUID and Riot ID, only after you run /lol vincular. Removed the moment you run /lol desvincular.
  • Live game data is reused for 60 seconds, then discarded, so the same game asked twice costs one request.
  • Champion images and icons come from Data Dragon and Community Dragon, not from the API.
  • Nothing is sold or shared. No ads, no tracking, no analytics on the bot or on this site.

Coming next: Champion win rate in ARAM for every player in a live game, read from each player’s recent matches (match-v5). Finished matches never change, so each one is read once and reused for everyone who played in it.
